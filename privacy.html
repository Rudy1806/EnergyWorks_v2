Privacy Policy

Energy Works respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through the Website, why we collect it, and how it's handled.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details: name, email address, phone number — collected when you book a consultation or contact us.
  • Booking details: preferred date/time, nature of consultation, and any site or utility information you choose to share for the audit.
  • Payment information: processed directly by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank account details on our servers — Razorpay handles this.
  • Communication data: messages exchanged with us over WhatsApp (via Meta's WhatsApp Business API), email, or our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to schedule, confirm, and remind you of consultations/audits (including via WhatsApp and email), process payments securely through Razorpay, respond to enquiries and provide support, improve our services and website performance, and meet legal and tax obligations.
