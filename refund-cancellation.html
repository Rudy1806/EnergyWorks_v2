Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to all consulting and energy audit engagements booked and paid for through the Energy Works website.

Cancellation Window

  • More than 48 hours before: Full refund.
  • Within 48 hours: Not eligible for refund; reschedule at discretion.
  • No-shows: Treated as completed and non-refundable.

How to Cancel or Reschedule

Send a cancellation or reschedule request to [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] or WhatsApp us at [YOUR BUSINESS WHATSAPP NUMBER], quoting your booking reference and payment ID. Requests are only valid once acknowledged by us in writing (email or WhatsApp).

Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method via Razorpay within 5–7 business days. Depending on your bank, the credit may take a few additional days to reflect in your account.
